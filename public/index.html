D&D Beyond to Alchemy Converter

Paste your D&D Beyond character's share link and click Convert. You'll get a file to import into Alchemy. Includes subclass features, homebrew, actions, weapon attacks and limited-use trackers.

    Private character or custom items?

    Share links only include public data, so D&D Beyond leaves out custom items and private characters. Use the bookmarklet (see README) while logged in, then upload the file it downloads:
